How to use the CSSBuy spreadsheet

  1. Open the CAFANS listing, find the original seller link if available, and confirm that CSSBuy can process it.
  2. Record the variant and size chart. For shirts and jackets, compare chest width and length with a garment you already own. For shoes, check whether the chart measures foot length or insole length.
  3. When warehouse QC photos are available, compare the size label, color, seams, and any visible marks with your order. Ask about exchange deadlines if anything differs.
  4. Compare delivery quotes for the same destination and parcel contents. Record the billable weight, packing fees, and whether each quote includes taxes or duties.

CSSBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the product links go?

They open CAFANS listings. Check the original seller URL and its availability through CSSBuy before placing an order.

What should I record in a landed-cost comparison?

Record the item price, domestic delivery, agent and packing fees, international shipping, and any applicable taxes or duties. Convert everything to one currency and count each charge once.

Will a product photo show the item that arrives at the warehouse?

A listing photo shows the seller’s advertised product. Warehouse QC photos, when available, let you check the item received for your order.
